A one-line entry for the make.fun bounty "One Line, One Minute: The Vending Machine Pickup Line", where an AI judge compares pickup lines a vending machine would use on a passing human, two at a time, and keeps the funnier one.

The line:

> Press my buttons, gorgeous — my coil never drops for anyone, but for you I'll fall completely, no exact change required.

The joke is built out of the machine's own world, nothing generic: its buttons, its coil, and the exact change it normally insists on. The contrast does the work — this coil drops for no one, yet for this passer-by it will "fall completely", the snack-drop and falling-for-you meanings landing in the same words, and the waived exact change seals it as a favour granted to one person only. 120 characters (122 bytes), one line, one breath, charming rather than pushy.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, no trailing newline). Running it twice produces byte-identical output.
